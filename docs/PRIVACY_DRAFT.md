# Privacy notice preparation

Internal draft; confirm the responsible company details, privacy contact, provider terms and retention arrangements before publication.

The contact form collects a name, email, optional company/phone, subject and message. The freight quote form collects company/contact details, cargo requirements, origin/destination, optional timing/handling information, clearing needs and notes.

When configured, these details are submitted directly to FormSubmit and forwarded to the approved company mailbox. There is no application database. FormSubmit, mailbox and hosting providers may retain records according to their arrangements; confirm the applicable terms and retention periods with the client. A successful provider response is not independent proof of inbox delivery.

Unsubmitted form entries remain in browser page memory and clear on refresh/navigation. No analytics or marketing subscriptions are added. OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles and receives ordinary browser requests; the site does not request the visitor's location.

Agree the production privacy contact, purposes, access controls, retention, request-handling process, providers and relevant jurisdictions with the client before publishing a final policy. Avoid requesting payment details or identity documents in free-text enquiries.
